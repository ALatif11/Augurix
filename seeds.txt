# Strategy families the researcher is seeded with, one per explore turn (prompt v12).
# Format: id | style (long, day or both) | name | mechanism | sources
#
# Rules for this list, fixed before any run used it:
# - Every source was published before 2023-01-01, the end of the discovery period, so no source can carry
#   information about the validation years (2023-2024) or the holdout (2025 on).
# - Every mechanism must be computable from one symbol's own hourly open, high, low, close, volume,
#   quote_volume and trades, long or flat. Cross-sectional findings are adapted to one asset and say so.
# - Families were chosen from the literature, not from gen-v1's results. Breakout and volume families are
#   included because they are standard in that literature, not because gen-v1's best scores were breakouts.
# - Several sources found the effect weak or gone after costs. They stay in: the judge decides, not the list.

tsmom | long | Time-series momentum | Be long while the asset's own return over the past one to four weeks is positive; crypto returns continue at weekly horizons. | Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012, JFE, "Time series momentum"; Liu & Tsyvinski 2021, RFS, "Risks and returns of cryptocurrency"
ma_ratio | long | Price relative to its moving average | Be long while the close is above its moving average over several days to a few months; the ratio of price to its average forecast daily bitcoin returns. | Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron 1992, JF; Detzel, Liu, Strauss, Zhou & Zhu 2021, Financial Management
ma_oscillator | long | Moving-average oscillator | Be long while a short moving average is above a long one; on bitcoin the buy signals of this rule earned more than its sell signals. | Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron 1992, JF; Corbet, Eraslan, Lucey & Sensoy 2019, Finance Research Letters
channel_breakout | long | Channel breakout | Enter when the close rises above the highest high of the previous N bars and exit when it falls below the lowest low of a shorter previous window. | Donchian channels; the Turtle trading rules (Faith 2007, "Way of the Turtle"); Brock et al. 1992 trading-range break; Hudson & Urquhart 2021, Annals of Operations Research
near_high | long | Nearness to the recent high | Be long while the close is near its highest level of the past several weeks; traders anchor on the recent high and under-react when price approaches it. | George & Hwang 2004, JF, "The 52-week high and momentum investing" (adapted to one asset and shorter windows)
vol_managed | long | Volatility-managed exposure | Size the position inversely to recent realised variance, cutting exposure when volatility is high; volatility persists but returns do not rise with it. | Moreira & Muir 2017, JF, "Volatility-managed portfolios"
volume_momentum | long | Momentum conditioned on volume | Combine a positive past return with trading volume measured against its own average; volume conditions how strong and how lasting a trend is. | Lee & Swaminathan 2000, JF, "Price momentum and trading volume"
attention | long | High-volume return premium | An unusually high volume or number of trades over a day or a week, relative to its own norm, is followed by higher returns as attention brings in buyers. | Gervais, Kaniel & Mingelgrin 2001, JF, "The high-volume return premium"; Liu & Tsyvinski 2021 (attention proxies) (adapted to one asset)
lottery | long | Avoiding lottery-like spikes | After an extreme positive return over an hour or a day, relative to recent volatility, the asset tends to do worse; stay out for a while after such spikes. | Bali, Cakici & Whitelaw 2011, JFE, "Maxing out"; Jia, Liu & Yan 2021, Finance Research Letters (cross-sectional; adapted to one asset; the sign is disputed in crypto)
skewness | long | Realised skewness | Realised skewness of recent hourly returns predicts later returns negatively; prefer to be long after negatively skewed periods. | Amaya, Christoffersen, Jacobs & Vasquez 2015, JFE; Jia, Liu & Yan 2021, Finance Research Letters (cross-sectional; adapted to one asset)
illiquidity | long | Illiquidity shocks | Illiquidity is the absolute return divided by quote volume; a sudden rise against its own average marks stress and lower returns at the time, a fall marks calm. | Amihud 2002, Journal of Financial Markets, "Illiquidity and stock returns" (adapted to one asset)
trend_strength | long | Trend-strength filter | Follow a trend only while directional movement is strong, measured from how far highs and lows extend beyond the previous bar's, smoothed over a day or more. | Wilder 1978, "New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems" (the ADX)
obv | long | Volume leading price | Cumulative volume signed by each bar's direction (on-balance volume) rising while price is flat signals accumulation before a move. | Granville 1963, "Granville's New Key to Stock Market Profits"
squeeze | both | Volatility squeeze | When the width of bands two standard deviations around a moving average is at a low against its own history, a large move tends to follow; enter on the break upward out of the bands. | Bollinger 2001, "Bollinger on Bollinger Bands"
trailing_stop | both | Volatility trailing stop as the exit | Any entry; exit when the close falls a multiple of the average true range below the highest close since entry, so the exit adapts to volatility. | Wilder 1978 (average true range); the chandelier exit of LeBeau, as described in Elder 2002, "Come Into My Trading Room"
intraday_momentum | day | Intraday momentum | The return over the first hours of the trading day predicts the return late in the same day; for crypto, use the UTC day or a major stock-market open as the start. | Gao, Han, Li & Zhou 2018, JFE, "Market intraday momentum"; Wen, Bouri, Xu & Zhao 2022, North American Journal of Economics and Finance
session_liquidity | day | Trading sessions | Bitcoin volume and volatility are highest around the European and US stock-market opens and lowest in the early UTC morning; time entries to the active sessions. | Eross, McGroarty, Urquhart & Wolfe 2019, Research in International Business and Finance; Baur, Cahill, Godfrey & Liu 2019, Finance Research Letters
day_of_week | day | Day of the week | Bitcoin's Monday returns were higher than other days and weekend volume lower; condition entries on the day of the week. | Caporale & Plastun 2019, Finance Research Letters, "The day of the week effect in the cryptocurrency market"; Baur et al. 2019 (who found such effects did not persist)
reversal | day | Short-term reversal as liquidity provision | Buy after a sharp fall over the past few hours, most of all when volume is low; the rebound is the pay for providing liquidity to forced sellers. | Lehmann 1990, QJE, "Fads, martingales, and market efficiency"; Jegadeesh 1990, JF; Bianchi, Babiak & Dickerson 2022, Journal of Banking and Finance
volume_return | day | Volume decides continuation or reversal | A large move on high volume tends to continue (informed trading) and one on low volume tends to reverse (liquidity trading); condition on the return and the volume together. | Llorente, Michaely, Saar & Wang 2002, RFS, "Dynamic volume-return relation of individual stocks"; Bianchi, Babiak & Dickerson 2022
overreaction | day | Moves after abnormal bars | After an hour whose move is several times its recent standard deviation, the next hours move further than usual; test trading in the direction of the abnormal move. | Caporale & Plastun 2019, Journal of Economic Studies, "Price overreactions in the cryptocurrency market" (found no profit after costs)
pullback | day | Short pullback inside an uptrend | Buy a short-term oversold reading (a 2- to 5-bar RSI or a run of down bars) only while the price is above a long moving average; exit on a short-term bounce. | Connors & Alvarez 2008, "Short Term Trading Strategies That Work"
opening_range | day | Opening-range breakout | Enter when the price breaks above the high of the first hours of the UTC day; exit by the end of the day. | Crabel 1990, "Day Trading with Short Term Price Patterns and Opening Range Breakout"
range_breakout | day | Volatility breakout | Enter when the price rises above the day's opening price plus a fraction of the previous day's high-low range; exit within the day. | Williams 1999, "Long-Term Secrets to Short-Term Trading"
vol_clustering | day | Volatility clustering | Volatility persists, so the last few hours' range forecasts the next few hours'; trade only when the expected move clears the 0.2% round-trip cost. | Engle 1982, Econometrica (ARCH); Clark 1973, Econometrica (volume and volatility move together)
vwap_reversion | day | Reversion to the volume-weighted price | A price well below the volume-weighted average price of the past several hours tends to revert toward it. | Practitioner execution benchmark; Berkowitz, Logue & Noser 1988, JF, "The total cost of transactions on the NYSE" (VWAP as the fair-price benchmark)
